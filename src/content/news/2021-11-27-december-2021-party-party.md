As if Libertarians need an excuse to party, this holiday season we’ve got 2!

In lieu of our Monthly Happy Hour Meeting, we’re celebrating a month of holidays and a birthday (some might say 2 birthdays).

**When:** Saturday, December 11, 2021 @ 7PM

**Where:** [Mister Tramps Sports Pub & Café](https://mistertramps.org) @ 8565 Research Blvd., Austin TX 78758
 in the private back room

**What:** Holiday Giving and Liberty Celebration

On December 11th the [Libertarian Party](https://www.lp.org/) turns 50 years old. That is a half-century milestone, the Golden Anniversary of the little party that could!

And December marks a month full of holidays, from Chanukah ending on the 6th to the Yule Winter Solstice on the 21st to Christmas on the 25th to Kwanzaa starting the 26th, and maybe more. It’s a time to remind us of caring & giving and creating peace & joy; quintessential libertarianism.

## Caring Libertarians

We’ll be collecting new and gently-used nonperishable items or unopened food items for contribution to one or more charities of our choice from anyone attending who may bring them. You choose the charity(s), and anyone wishing to make room in their homes for new gifts can bring things to donate.

## Yankee-Swap

We’ll be sharing liberty-themed gifts among one another, for anyone who’d like to shop for that (not-too-expensive) special symbol of freedom that someone else may use and enjoy, sort of a Libertarian Secret Santa.

Wondering what to get? Some Suggestions:

- T-shirts, yard signs, bumper stickers, and more from the [Libertarian Booster PAC](https://www.libertarianboosterpac.org/shop.html#/)
- Completely legal CBD and Delta-8 THC products from [Nugg of Knowledge](https://nuggofknowledge.com/shop-nugg/)
- A variety of cool stuff from [Pound Town](https://www.poundtowntx.com/p/cool-merch-here.html)
- Books by the [Tuttle Twins](https://tuttletwins.com/products/)

What are your ideas & sources?

## Liberty Giving

Libertarians are charitable so people don’t have to feel stuck depending on government. Not to mention giving gives us the warm fuzzies. So what, if anything, can you contribute, and to where?

So come as you are, or in your dressiest dress or ugliest sweater, bring friends, and bear gifts or not!

Come eat, drink, and be merry with us in a room full of liberty-minded fun folks!
