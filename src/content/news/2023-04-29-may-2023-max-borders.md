May’s meeting will feature special guest speaker **Max Borders**, plus legislative updates possibly from LPTexas Legislative Coordinator Kevin Hale and/or activist & cannabis advocate Elizabeth Miller.

![](/images/2023/04/max-borders-1.jpg)

Max is a prolific writer & speaker and theorist & futurist, as well as serial entrepreneur & generous philanthropist. Among other ventures & endeavors, he’s the former Editor & Content Director at [Foundation for Economic Education](https://fee.org/people/max-borders/) (FEE) and current Executive Director of [Social Evolution](https://social-evolution.com/), and co-founded experiential events such as [Future Frontiers](http://futurefrontiers.co/) (preceded by Voice & Exit).

Also an author, he’ll be available for book signing and may have a few tomes onhand for sale if you don’t have any yet.

*And ask him about the **$20,000 Constitution writing contest**!*
