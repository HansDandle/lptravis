*NOT to be confused with "2020, too"*

**When**: Monday, January 10, 2022 @ 6:30-9PM
**Where**: [Casa Chapala Mexican Cuisine & Tequila Bar](https://www.casachapala.com/location/austin/)
**What**: New Year News, Guest Speaker, Candidates, and Party

LP Travis is starting the new year loaded with news, candidates, parties, and more great guests:

![](/images/2022/01/andrew-withers-avatar-2.jpg)

The LP-UK was founded on January 1, 2008. So it celebrated its 14th birthday 14 years ago on New Years Day 2022!
[Andrew P. Withers](https://andrewpwithers.wordpress.com/) is stopping by on his visit from across the pond where he is a founding member of the [Libertarian Party of the United Kingdom](https://libertarianpartyuk.com/) (LP-UK) to say a few words about the condition of freedom in Great Britain. (Let’s just say it’s good that we broke off from those fellows back in 1776!)

![](/images/2022/01/texas-libertarian-candidates-1.jpg)

[Candidates](https://www.libertarianboosterpac.org/2022-texas-candidate-list.html#/) will be coming to talk about their runs for office, answer questions, and we hope leave with more supporters for their respective races.

![](/images/2022/01/tg-group-cover-2-sq.jpg)

The [Board of Directors](https://votetexasgold.org/about/#board) of [Texas Gold](https://votetexasgold.org/) will announce that [tickets](https://votetexasgold.org/product/texas-turns-50/) have become available for the [50th Birthday Party](https://votetexasgold.org/product/texas-turns-50/) it’s throwing for the [Libertarian Party of Texas](https://www.lptexas.org/home) later this month on its actual Golden Anniversary, along with some perks for those who [register](https://votetexasgold.org/product/texas-turns-50/), and other news about this momentous milestone occasion.

Come start the new year on a high note with us to hear & talk about issues of the day and meet more local Libertarians on the 10th day of this brand new year!
