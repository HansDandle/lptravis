The Libertarian Party is a convention party in Texas and does not hold a primary election. Instead, we hold a county convention to elect officers and nominate candidates for public office.

This is an official public announcement of the dates and locations of our precinct and county conventions.

PRECINCT CONVENTION:

Tuesday, March 10, 2026, 7:00 p.m.

Casa Chapala Restaurant

9041 Research Blvd. Austin, TX 78758

COUNTY CONVENTION:

Saturday, March 14, 2026, 10:30 a.m.

Austin Library – Yarborough Branch

2200 Hancock Drive, Austin, TX 78756

For further information, contact Vice Chair Ted Brown at (626) 429-1661, or tbrown@lptexas.org.
