We are pleased to present special guest **[Dr. Mary Ruwart, Ph. D.](https://www.facebook.com/maryjruwart)**, who will speak on **“*Do Vaccine Mandates Protect our Health or Threaten our Freedoms*?”**

![Dr. Mary Ruwart](/images/2021/11/mary-ruwart-28234957_10155056060297121_4427022319869508492_o.jpg)

Dr. Ruwart has a Ph. D. in biophysics. She spent 19 years as a pharmaceutical research scientist for [Upjohn Pharmaceuticals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Upjohn) and has written extensively on the subject of government regulation of the [drug industry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drug_industry). She has also written books on communicating Libertarian ideas, such as “Healing Our World.”

Dr. Ruwart’s Libertarian activism is widespread. In Texas, she has run for U. S. Senator and State Comptroller. She has sought the Libertarian Party’s presidential nomination. She is currently chair of the party’s Judicial Committee. On the international front, she is chair of Liberty International, which brings together libertarians and fellow travelers from around the world.
